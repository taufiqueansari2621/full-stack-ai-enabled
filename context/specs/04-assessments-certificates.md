# Unit 04: Assessments and Certificates

## Goal

Add a real evidence chain from learning activity through assessment and into a clearly scoped local Certificate of Completion.

## Behavior

- Provide a dedicated foundation assessment with ten concept-aligned questions.
- Allow answer changes before submission and require every question.
- Persist score, accuracy, weak topics, and attempt history per local learner.
- Show explanations for correct and incorrect answers and recommend revision after failure.
- Require all six foundation lessons and an assessment score of at least 80% before certificate issuance.
- Persist a unique local credential and support browser print / Save PDF.
- Clearly state that the credential is a local Certificate of Completion, not external accreditation.

## Verification

- Browser flow completes and persists a 100% assessment.
- Certificate remains unavailable before lesson requirements are met.
- Eligible certificate issuance and persistence are verified.
- Reset clears assessment and certificate data for the active learner.
